# Refund guide

How support issues a refund from the console. Every refund follows
the [refund window](#window).

<toc depth={2} />

## Before you start

You need the **Refunds** role, under *Settings → Roles*.

<warning>A refund can't be undone once the payment provider confirms it.</warning>

## Disputes

A [dispute](#dispute) locks the payment until the provider resolves it.

~~~heron
dispute: term =| A customer's challenge of a payment through their bank.
~~~
